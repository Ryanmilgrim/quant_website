Investment Universe – Benchmark Composition

Select the assets that form your investment universe. This will anchor your benchmark portfolio and downstream performance comparisons.

Practitioners use industry portfolios to benchmark sector rotation strategies, attribute performance to industry tilts, and stress-test allocation across market cycles. This module defines the asset universe that anchors downstream style and risk analysis.

Fama-French daily industry portfolios provide long, academically vetted history. Log returns keep compounding consistent. Benchmarks are value-weighted; switch to log growth to spot long-run compounding.

Data & Methodology ▼
Fama-French Industry Portfolios

Kenneth French maintains daily and monthly returns for value-weighted industry portfolios constructed from CRSP data. Each portfolio groups NYSE, AMEX, and NASDAQ stocks by SIC code into N industry buckets. Available granularities: 5, 10, 12, 17, 30, 38, and 48 industries.

Log Returns

This module displays log (continuously compounded) returns rather than simple returns. For a price series \(P_t\), the log return is \(r_t = \ln(P_t / P_{t-1})\). Log returns are additive over time, making multi-period compounding straightforward:

\[\sum r_t = \ln(P_T / P_0)\]
Indexed Growth

The "indexed price growth" view compounds returns from a base of 100. The "log price growth" view shows the cumulative sum of log returns, which is equivalent to \(\ln(\text{Index}_T / 100)\). Both views tell the same compounding story; the log view linearizes exponential growth for easier visual comparison.

Value Weighting

Portfolios are value-weighted (market-cap weighted), so each stock contributes in proportion to its market capitalization. This reflects the investable opportunity set and is the natural benchmark for institutional portfolios.
